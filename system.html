The System·Detect and respond

Orbit to a Guardian in 30 minutes.

Most satellite programmes count fires after they start. SATARK's CaViT model reads satellites and field sensors together and flags a fire 14 days before it starts, then a trained person reaches the farmer before the match is lit.

The name is the spec

Satellite-Aided Thermal Anomaly Response Kit.

Six letters, six jobs. Each one is a stage that has to work in a block where the internet is patchy and nobody has time for an app.

SSatellite

INSAT-3DR and GK-2A imagery every 15 minutes, far more often than a daily polar pass.

AAided

Our CaViT model, a cascaded vision transformer, for 200× upscaling.

TThermal

Heat mapped onto real crop boundaries, plot by plot.

AAnomaly

Land use filtering that throws out heat that is not a farm.

RResponse

Telegram alerts to the Guardian network.

KKit

Open source, free to use, built for India.

The logic loop

Five steps from a hot pixel to a person in the field.

Every stage passes a smaller, cleaner message to the next one. By the time it reaches a Guardian it is a pin on a map and a reason to go.

1Every 15 min

Satellite detection

Geostationary feeds watch the block all day, where NASA's VIIRS passes once.

2≈120 ms

CaViT upscaling

Blurry coarse pixels are rebuilt into fine coordinates in three steps.

3Land use check

Precision filter

Detections are checked against land use maps. Kilns, chimneys and roads are dropped.

4Telegram bot · seconds

Telegram alert

A Telegram bot sends the nearest Guardian the plot, a map pin and the farmer's context. Light on data, fine on a basic Android phone.

5On the ground

Situational action

Machinery, a guided controlled burn or immediate extinguishing, chosen from local conditions.

Early warning

Read the field before anyone reaches for a match.

Paddy changes colour from orbit as it ripens. SATARK tracks the vegetation index (NDVI) of each field. When a field crosses from green crop toward brown stubble, the clock starts.

That gives the block 14 days to move a baler, book a CHC machine or send a Guardian with subsidy forms. Prevention happens while the crop is still standing.

The AI engine

Three small jumps beat one big guess.

Asking a model to jump straight from a kilometre pixel to a single plot invites it to invent detail. SATARK climbs the resolution ladder in three steps, MODIS to Landsat-8 to Sentinel-2, so each step stays grounded in real data.

CaViT · cascaded SwinIR transformer

The model learned fire on California's largest wildfire, then was adapted with Bengal land cover so it recognises a paddy plot, a pond and a brick kiln for what they are. The domain adaptation followed guidance from an IIT Kharagpur advisor.

SENTRY Mk I · ground nodes

Satellites see heat. The ground knows the wind.

Solar powered micro-climate stations sit on village schools and government buildings. They read the air that decides what a Guardian should do: whether a guided burn is safe or the fire has to go out now.

Board
ESP32 class nodes, Heltec LoRa32 V3 radios
Senses
Gas, humidity, local wind and temperature inversions
Power
Solar, on school and block buildings
Network
LoRa store and forward to a master node. A Guardian's phone hotspot named SATARK_GUARDIAN collects the day's data.
Pilot grid
50 nodes in Kolaghat Block, authorized by the BDO in February 2026
Licence
Open hardware, CERN-OHL-S-2.0

A mobile cousin, PATHIK, clips ESP32 and BME680 sensors with GPS onto Guardians' motorcycles to map heat stress and particulate matter across rural Bengal as they ride.

The 4-Step Shield

Support first. Enforcement last.

Technology finds the field. People decide how the farmer is treated. The Shield is the order in which SATARK shows up.

1

Orbital early warning

Crop shift tracking flags fields 14 days out, so balers and CHC machines are booked before the harvest window opens.

2

Friendly voice first

Before harvest, Guardians bring soil health cards and subsidy forms. The first contact is help, which is what earns the second one.

3

Ground Guardian dispatch

If a field is flagged, the nearest Guardian gets a Telegram alert and goes. Where no machine is free, the Guardian guides a safer, smaller burn using SENTRY's wind data.

4

The Trust Card

Cooperation is recorded. Farmers who work with the programme get priority for government machinery and subsidies, so not burning pays.
