Government mandate · the actual letters

From fine to forecast.

The block office that used to record fires and issue fines now lends its rooftops, schools and village heads to prevent them. Here are the letters as they were issued, with what each one says.

Kolaghat Development BlockRef · letter of interest, 13 Jan 2026

Authorization for Phase-2 pilot deployment of Project SATARK, and student skill development.

  1. Approval for the Phase-2 field deployment of SATARK, “a hyper-local IoT-based pollution and anomaly monitoring grid”.
  2. A 50-node pilot grid across Kolaghat Block.
  3. Permission to survey and mount LoRaWAN sensor nodes on the rooftops of Gram Panchayat offices, flood shelters and other state buildings.
  4. High schools with Atal Tinkering Labs asked to help form a Student Technical Team for device assembly and maintenance.
  5. Zero financial liability for the block. The block office gets continuous access to the live dashboard.
  6. Gram Panchayat Pradhans and school authorities asked to cooperate.

Trust audit · 2025 to 2026

Nobody had told them the machines existed.

Before writing a line of code, the team surveyed 34 farming households in the pilot area. Not one knew there was a subsidised machinery centre nearby. The district has 36 of them.

The Positive Deviance and Trust Gap Study also found neighbours who already manage straw without burning. SATARK's job is partly technology and partly making sure the next farmer hears about what already works.

0/34
Household that did not knowCustom Hiring Centre in the district

We asked them why

They weren't burning out of ignorance. They were burning out of arithmetic.

The field team went where the smoke was. Not to tell farmers what they were doing wrong, but to ask them why. The answer never changed: nothing clears a field inside the window.

That answer is why SATARK builds DIVA. Not to ask farmers to choose better, but to give them something better to choose.

The combustion paradox

The match wins on price, every time.

Farmers who burn are not careless. They are doing arithmetic. Any fix that costs more than a matchstick has to be paid for by someone other than the farmer.

The Guardian network

Thirty students who know every bund by name.

Field Guardians are local students trained to verify alerts, talk to farmers first and log what they find. Alerts reach them on Telegram. They are the reason an alert turns into a conversation instead of a fine.
