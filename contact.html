Open positions
Four roles, in the order we need them.

      
All part time, roughly 10 to 15 hours a week, with a three month minimum. Especially if you live with this air every winter. Tap a role to read it.

    

    

      

        01Field CoordinatorPurba Medinipur · On ground · Bengali required
        

          
Why this role exists
Everything we publish rests on data collected from fields in one block, and right now that depends on very few people. Sensors need placing and checking. Farmers need visiting more than once. Trust is built by turning up, not by explaining.
This is the role the whole project leans on, and the one we are most serious about filling.

          
You should have
  • Fluent Bengali. Not conversational, fluent.
  • Ability to travel regularly within Purba Medinipur.
  • Willingness to spend long days outdoors, in season, in heat.
  • Patience with people who are sceptical of strangers carrying equipment.
  • An agriculture background helps but is not required.
  • A two wheeler and licence would make your life easier.

          
Apply for this role 

        

      

      

        02Founder's Office InternHybrid · Generalist · Remote possible
        

          
Why this role exists
There is more work than there are people, and it does not sort itself neatly into departments. One week it is a grant application, the next it is cleaning sensor data, the next it is preparing a brief for a block officer.
You sit next to the founder and take whatever the project needs that week. Range over depth, for now.

          
You should have
  • Clear, quick writing in English. This matters more than anything else.
  • Comfort with being handed a problem and no instructions.
  • The ability to read a technical paper in an unfamiliar field without freezing.
  • Working spreadsheets. Nothing fancy.
  • Bengali or Hindi is a real advantage.
  • Python, GIS or electronics are a bonus, not a requirement.

          
Apply for this role 

        

      

      

        03Content and Digital PresenceRemote · Writing plus web · One combined role
        

          
Why this role exists
Our work lives in decay curves and thermal data, and none of that reads to anyone outside the project. It has to be translated three ways: an officer wants one page and a number, a funder wants the mechanism, a farmer wants to know what the box on the pole does.
You also keep the surface coherent, so the dataset, preprints, certifications and this site all point at each other.

          
You should have
  • Something you have written that a stranger read voluntarily.
  • Confidence editing HTML and CSS. This site has no CMS behind it.
  • An eye for the sentence that is doing PR instead of communicating.
  • A habit of noticing a stale figure, a dead link, two versions of one claim.
  • Bengali or Hindi writing is a strong plus.
  • SEO, Git or basic video editing are bonuses.

          
Apply for this role 

        

      

      

        04Hardware and ElectronicsIn person required · Bench work · High voltage
        

          
Why this role exists
DIVA is the largest gap between what we are building and what we can currently prove. Electrode geometry, power supply and drum mechanics all need someone at a bench with instruments, not someone reviewing drawings remotely.
Our sensor nodes also need iteration once they have been out in weather for a season.

          
You should have
  • Built something physical that worked. Not a kit you assembled.
  • Comfort with basic bench instruments.
  • The habit of documenting what you build so it survives you leaving.
  • Respect for safety. We generate plasma. Carelessness rules you out.
  • High voltage experience is rare and extremely welcome.
  • KiCad and ESP32 firmware are both useful here.

          
Apply for this role

Questions

    
Before you write.

    

    
What is SATARK, in one line?
A system that spots a likely crop fire from orbit, sends a trained local student to the farmer before the match is lit, and, with DIVA, removes the reason to burn at all.
Where does SATARK work today?
Kolaghat Block and Egra in Purba Medinipur, West Bengal. By June 2026 the Guardians were working with 37 farming families across eight villages.
Which government offices back SATARK?
The Block Development Officer, Kolaghat (letter of interest, 13 January 2026, and Phase-2 pilot authorization, 26 February 2026) and the Assistant Director of Agriculture, Egra (technical validation). The SDO, Egra, supports the programme. You can read all three letters on the Field page.
Does SATARK report or fine farmers?
No. The system is built on support first: early warning, subsidy forms, machinery bookings and a Trust Card that rewards cooperation. Enforcement is the government's job, and the block's own letters point the network toward prevention.
What does it cost a farmer or the block?
Nothing for detection and alerts. The BDO's authorization runs at zero financial liability to the block. DIVA is meant to reach farmers through Custom Hiring Centres under the CRM subsidy, at about ₹150 an acre.
Can my block or district run SATARK?
That is the goal of Phase 3. Use the form above or write to team@satark.co.in with your block, the crop calendar and who in the administration is involved, and we will set up a call.
How does an alert reach a Guardian?
Through a Telegram bot. The nearest Guardian gets the plot, a map pin and the farmer's context, and replies from the same chat when they are on the way and when it is resolved.
Is the 10 day DIVA number proven?
Not yet, and we say so. It comes from our decay model fitted to published measurements. The Year 1 field pilot is what will test it.
Can I buy a DIVA unit?
Not yet. The prototype is being built for field trials and for the LPU Bharat Innovation Challenge in November 2026. Request a demo and we will tell you when units are available for Custom Hiring Centres.
Is it really open source?
Yes. Hardware is certified by OSHWA as IN000084 under CERN-OHL-S-2.0, software is GPL-3.0-or-later and documents are CC BY-SA 4.0. Ask the team for the repositories and CAD files.
Who funds SATARK?
The founder is an Emergent Ventures fellow, part of the India 19th cohort. Farmers pay nothing, and the block carries no cost.
I am a student. Can I become a Guardian?
If you live in or near a pilot block, yes. Guardians today come from five schools in Purba Medinipur. Use the form above, pick Student, and tell us your school. Training happens before the harvest season.
Are the roles paid, and how much time do they take?
All four open roles are part time, about 10 to 15 hours a week, for at least three months. Ask about compensation for a specific role when you apply and you will get a straight answer.
I am a journalist. Who do I talk to?
Write to team@satark.co.in or use the form and pick Press. We can arrange interviews, field visits in season and photos from the pilot.
