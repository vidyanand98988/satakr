The payment chain

We don't need farmers to pay for this.

The Crop Residue Management scheme already subsidises anti-stubble-burning machinery at 50% for individual farmers and 80% for Custom Hiring Centres. ₹3,623 crore deployed since 2018, about 2.95 lakh machines, roughly 40,000 CHCs across Punjab, Haryana and UP.

Implementation

Purba Medinipur first. Then the rice-wheat belt.

Short term: a validated pilot, a CRM listing filed, manufacturing locked. Long term: fleet standard in India, then every rice-wheat system that shares our window.

Year 1

Full-season pilot in Purba Medinipur. Batch A/B validation in the field.

Year 2

CRM listing secured and a first CHC. Manufacturing partner and volume.

Year 3

Punjab and Haryana entry.

Year 5

Multi-state. Carbon revenue begins.

Year 8+

Pakistan, Vietnam, Philippines, Indonesia. OEM factory-fit as standard.

What has to be true

The four bets behind the numbers.

Every projection on this page depends on these. We would rather show them than hide them.

Volume fabrication brings COGS to ₹20,000 to 23,000.

Today's BOM is one-off job-shop pricing on the rolled shell, machined polymer and glass sleeves. Material dominates at volume. This is the single biggest driver of margin.

Unproven at volume

DIVA makes the CRM approved machinery list.

The whole distribution thesis. The existing BDO, ADA and SDO mandates are the pathway. The listing is not granted yet.

Customers and outreach

Who buys, who validates.

Partner selection rules: already inside CRM infrastructure, running combine fleets in paddy-wheat rotation with a sowing window under 20 days, and in West Bengal, an existing government relationship. Expand west from there.

Buyer

Custom Hiring Centres

80% CRM subsidy. DIVA is a ₹7,000 add-on to machines they already run.

Buyer

Progressive farmers

The 50% subsidy tier. Faster sales cycle, no CHC gatekeeping.

Payer

State agriculture departments

Need CAQM compliance without penalising farmers.

Validator

ICAR and IARI Pusa

DIVA makes the Pusa Decomposer hit the window.

Outreach runs through state agriculture departments in Punjab, Haryana and UP, CHC federations and ICAR or PAU field days where CHC operators already gather; validators such as IARI Pusa and the CII Foundation, whose 1,000-village network cut burning from 64% to 24% in intervened districts; and the Guardian network, which doubles as demo infrastructure. Partners we are working toward include ICAR, IRRI, CGIAR, FAO, ADB, NITI Aayog, CII and nurture.farm. None is a confirmed partner yet.
