Purba Medinipur, West Bengal

A district that didn't burn five years ago is burning now. Purba Medinipur. Not Punjab. Nobody was looking here. SATARK was built to see it coming. Satellites, field sensors and our CaViT model flag a fire 14 days before it starts. Then we hit the thing nobody warns you about. A prediction does not stop a fire.

Scroll14 days

So we asked them why

The field team went where the smoke was. Not to tell farmers what they were doing wrong, but to ask them why. The answer never changed. Nothing clears a field inside the window.

They weren't burning out of ignorance. They were burning out of arithmetic.

₹600A day of manual clearing labour. Most farmers cannot afford it.
₹1One matchstick. The only technology inside their budget and inside their window.

So SATARK is building the answer: DIVA, cold plasma pretreatment made for the 14 days farmers actually have. Not to ask farmers to choose better, but to give them something better to choose.

The true cost

Farmers are not the problem. 14 days is.

Of India's 500 million tonnes of crop residue every year, about 100 million tonnes are burnt, because nothing else fits the window.

44,000 to 98,000

Premature deaths every year in India from crop residue burning. Children under five in high-burning districts face three times the risk of acute respiratory infection.

+75%

Rise in CO₂e from residue burning in one decade.

40%

Of India's entire annual air quality damage from residue burning comes from just six districts in Punjab.

Everything has been tried

Five serious fixes. Every one runs out of the same 14 days.

Innovators kept fighting the symptom, not the root. Turn the ring with the arrows or drag it.Swipe through the cards.

1

Happy Seeder and Super Seeder

Sows wheat straight through standing stubble. Needs a 45+ HP tractor. Free or ₹100 to 200 an hour through a CHC, ₹800 to 1,000 an acre privately.

25 to 35 day window · CHC queues
2

Baling and biomass plants

Straw is baled and sold to coal plants for co-firing. Renting a baler costs ₹1,500 to 3,000 an acre.

Tenders misaligned · bales abandoned
3

Compressed biogas

Straw turned to methane by anaerobic digestion. Capacity exists on paper. PEDA logs 6 of 57 commissioned projects actually running.

About 10% of plants running
4

Satellite monitoring and fines

Satellite tracking paired with NGT fines of ₹5,000 to 30,000. Over ₹2.3 crore collected in 2024.

Farmers bypass satellite timing
5

Pusa Bio-Decomposer

A microbial spray that rots stubble in the field. Decomposition in 15 to 20 days against 40 to 60 naturally, and an 8 to 10% wheat yield gain.

20 to 25 days to field ready

The innovation · DIVA

Ten days.
They had fourteen.

Cold plasma, delivered by DIVA. An electric field strong enough to break the wax in seconds, cool enough that nothing burns.

  • Self-extinguishingMicrodischarges form and die in nanoseconds, never reaching combustion temperature.
  • Breaks the wax on contactReactive oxygen and nitrogen species, energetic electrons and UV oxidise the wax and cutin.
  • Lives inside the harvesterNo extra pass, no extra fuel, no extra day.

One system, three layers

See it from orbit. Reach the farmer. Remove the reason to burn.

DIVA is the last layer of a bigger system. Detection finds the field, people earn the trust, and the machine makes not burning the easy choice.

01 · Detect

CaViT

See it from orbit

Satellites and field sensors feed our CaViT model, which flags a fire 14 days before it starts and sharpens coarse thermal pixels down to a single plot.

The system 02 · Respond

Guardians

Reach the farmer first

A trained student Guardian gets a Telegram alert with the plot and a map pin. The first visit brings subsidy forms and machine bookings, before anyone talks about fines.

The field 03 · Treat

DIVA

Remove the reason to burn

DIVA rides on the combine and hits the straw with cold plasma. The waxy skin cracks, microbes get in, and the field is ready inside the window.

Impact, per unit, per season

We don't need farmers to pay for this.

The Crop Residue Management scheme already pays 80% of anti-stubble-burning machinery for Custom Hiring Centres. A CHC pays about ₹7,000 for DIVA and earns it back in the first two days of harvest.
