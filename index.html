About Diva

A cold plasma drum built for the rice harvest.

Every winter, leftover straw is burned across the Indo-Gangetic Plain because burning is the only option most smallholders can afford. The smoke settles over the region for weeks.

Diva gives farmers another way. The drum fits the back of a combine harvester. As the crop is cut, straw passes through rows of plasma electrode tubes and is spread back on the field, so there is nothing left to light.

How it works

Fits the back of a combine and works in the same pass.

Collect

Straw goes straight into the drum

The harvester cuts the crop and feeds the leftover straw into Diva.

Treat

Cold plasma, driven by the harvester

A belt from the harvester turns the drum while the electrode tubes treat the straw.

Return

Back on the field, not burned

Treated straw is spread behind the machine instead of being set alight.

Benefits

What Diva does in the field.

Cold plasma, no flame

High voltage across glass barriers creates a cold plasma. No fire, no smoke and no open flame in the field.

Treats straw as you harvest

The drum mounts at the back of the combine, so straw is treated in the same pass that cuts the crop.

Built for field conditions

A stainless steel housing and a sealed power connector are designed for dust, chaff and long harvest days.

Straw stays on the field

Treated straw is spread back across the field instead of being burned, so there is nothing left to light.

Cleaner air for the region

Every field that is not burned means less smoke trapped over the plain through the winter months.

Why it matters

Burning is the cheapest choice a farmer has.
