Founder · CEO · Project Director

Sk Reezaal Arafat

From Gurchakli, Purba Medinipur. Building climate and technology projects since 2022. Founded FTOH, designed SATARK's system and its path to government authorization, and leads the DIVA hardware work.

On 23 February 2026, an IAS officer in Egra asked me where I graduated from. Kaha se B.Tech kiya ho. I hadn't. I was in Class 10. Purba Medinipur didn't burn five years ago. Now it does, and I live here. I built the prototype myself and published every dataset in the open, because this sector runs on claims nobody can check. That was the day I graduated. Not from a college. From watching this problem to executing on it.
Emergent Ventures India · 19th cohort OSHWA IN000084 The Better India, Oct 2026

Four tracks

How the work is split.

Every person sits in one track and borrows from the others. The tracks map to the layers of the system.

TRACK 1

Field Guardians

Verification, farmer conversations, Trust Cards and ground truth for the model.

TRACK 2

Hardware and IoT

SENTRY nodes, PATHIK units and the DIVA plasma drum.

TRACK 3

Narrative, strategy, research

Field reports, academic publishing, grants and government relations.

TRACK 4

Software and vision AI

The SwinIR pipeline, databases, dispatch and the full technical stack.
