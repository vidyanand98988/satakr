Journey·2022 to now

From a village seminar to a plasma drum.

SATARK grew out of FTOH, a youth-led climate collective in Purba Medinipur. It started as a question about satellites and fire. It became a system, a mandate and a machine.

Founded FTOH · 2022SATARK 2025DIVA 2026
2022
The start

FTOH is founded

Sk Reezaal Arafat starts building climate and technology projects and founds FTOH, For The Only Home, as a youth-led climate collective.

Early years

CLI'MATE' seminars

Community seminars on climate in the villages. The conversations with farmers that would later shape SATARK start here.

2025
2025

SATARK begins

A question about satellites becomes a system: can a crop fire be stopped before the match is lit, without fining the farmer?

2025 to 2026

The trust audit

34 farming households surveyed. None knew a subsidised machinery centre was nearby. The Positive Deviance and Trust Gap Study is written.

2025 to 2026

Research goes public

Field data on Harvard Dataverse, two SSRN preprints and a Zenodo archive. The AI model is trained on 32,000+ wildfire images and adapted for Bengal.

2026
13 Jan 2026

Letter of interest from BDO Kolaghat

The block says it will observe the pilot in the harvest season and use the live data to send balers where they are needed.

13 Feb 2026

Technical validation from ADA Egra

The ground sensor method is found “technically sound and practically viable”, and one pilot unit is authorized in Egra Sub-division.

23 Feb 2026

"Kaha se B.Tech kiya ho?"

An IAS officer in Egra asks Reezaal where he graduated from. He hadn't. He was in Class 10, with a working system and a field team behind him.

26 Feb 2026

Phase-2 authorization from BDO Kolaghat

A 50-node grid on Gram Panchayat offices and flood shelters, a Student Technical Team in Atal Tinkering Lab schools, zero cost to the block. The first SATARK website, Page 0, goes live the same day.

20 Mar 2026

OSHWA certification

SATARK hardware is certified open source as IN000084, under CERN-OHL-S-2.0 and GPL-3.0-or-later.

July 2026

DIVA is born

The missing piece: a way to remove the reason to burn. A cold plasma unit cell is designed to crack the waxy skin of rice straw.

August 2026

The wafer works. The chamber is drawn.

The bench unit is built, wired and powered in Gurchakli, and the discharge is confirmed. The spoke wheel is dropped for a 500 mm chamber with ten fixed axial electrodes.

September 2026

The model and the fix

A two-pool decay model fitted to 19 published measurements (R² = 0.84). An insulation flaw in the electrode stack is found and resolved.

19 Sep 2026

Emergent Ventures India, 19th cohort

Tyler Cowen names Project SATARK in the cohort announcement on Marginal Revolution: “a decentralized agricultural fire-interception grid”.

Read the post
12 Oct 2026

Featured by The Better India

A Young Achievers feature on Reezaal and SATARK, written by Nishtha Kawrani, later republished by DailyGood.

Read the feature
Now · October 2026
20 to 21 Nov 2026

LPU Bharat Innovation Challenge

The DIVA prototype, the app and the pitch go to Punjab, in the heart of India's stubble burning belt.

Winter 2026 target

Phase 3, full scale

Expansion across the Indo-Gangetic Plain, ISRO Bhuvan integration, Trust Cards as a subsidy metric, and an open-source release for the Mekong Delta.

The FTOH family

SATARK is not the only thing we build.

FTOH builds early warning and sensing for places too small to be anyone's business case. The projects share people, hardware and a way of working.

Mobile sensing

PATHIK

ESP32 and BME680 sensors on Field Guardians' motorcycles log heat stress and particulate matter across rural Bengal as they ride.

Heat

HEATWATCH

Wet-bulb heat stress mapping to find extreme heat zones and route gig workers around them.

Floods

PRABAH

Flood prediction, alerts and monitoring for West Bengal districts, built on regional hydrological data.

Community

CLI'MATE'

The village climate seminars where FTOH began, and where the first questions about burning were asked.
